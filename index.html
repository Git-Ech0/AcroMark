Keyboard shortcuts

Click the pencil to change a shortcut — press any key to set it, Esc to cancel.

With the Select tool, click a shape to move it, drag any of its points to reshape it, or click the red badge to delete it. Curved lines have a third point in the middle — drag it to bend the curve. Double-click (or double-tap) text to edit it, and use the arrow keys to nudge. Hold Shift while drawing a line, arrow, curve, rectangle or circle to snap the angle or make it square. Changing the colour or size while something is selected restyles it. Right-click any mark, in any tool, for Duplicate / Bring to Front / Send to Back / Delete. Hold the middle mouse button anywhere to pan without switching tools. On touch screens, use the Pan tool to scroll the page. The Detect & Edit Text tool reads the PDF's real text and turns it into click-to-edit boxes styled to match — pick a line or paragraph, type over it, and the original text underneath is replaced. It also finds answers typed on top of the page by annotation tools such as Kami; replacing one removes the original from the saved PDF.
