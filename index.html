Keyboard shortcuts

With the Select tool, click a shape to move it, drag any of its points to reshape it, or click the red badge to delete it. Curved lines have a third point in the middle — drag it to bend the curve. Double-click (or double-tap) text to edit it, and use the arrow keys to nudge. Hold Shift while drawing a line, arrow, curve, rectangle or circle to snap the angle or make it square. Changing the colour or size while something is selected restyles it. On touch screens, use the Pan tool to scroll the page.
